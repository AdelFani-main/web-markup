Popular fixes

Start with the problems that show up most often. These are the small issues that usually have a simple cause.

Beginner step 1

Shifting feels rough or unreliable

Check cable tension, indexing, and limit screws.

Beginner step 2

Brakes squeal or rub

Clean the pads, align the caliper, and check rotor wear.

Beginner step 3

Fix a flat tire

Remove the wheel carefully, replace the tube, and check the tyre for damage.
