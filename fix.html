Fix The Folding Bicycle Hinge

Step-by-step repair guide for a folding bike hinge that squeaks, binds, or won’t align.

Warning / Safety

  • Wear gloves and eye protection.
  • Support the bike securely before working.
  • Do not force misaligned hinge parts—check for debris or bent hardware.
  • If cracks or severe damage are found, stop and replace affected components.

5-Step Timeline

Follow them in order. Each one checks a different part of the problem.

1) Identify the Problem

Listen and check the alignment

Look for squeaks, grinding, wobble, or a hinge that won’t fold cleanly. Check the hinge pins, bushings, and nearby bolts for looseness or wear.

2) Clean the hinge area

Get rid of dirt and old grime

Wipe away the dirt around the hinge and remove debris from the small gaps. Clean the pin and bushing contact points until the movement feels smooth and easy.

3) Check for worn parts

Inspect pins, bushings, and hardware

Look closely for worn hinge pins, cracked bushings, or damaged washers. If something is worn out, replace it before reassembling the hinge.

4) Lubricate and reassemble

Use a light hand and line everything up

Add just a small amount of the correct lubricant to the hinge contact points. Reassemble the parts in the right order and make sure they line up neatly.

5) Test the fit

Check smooth movement and a secure hold

Open and fold the hinge a few times. Watch for binding, uneven gaps, or loose fasteners. Tighten anything that feels off and adjust until it moves cleanly.
