Fix Your Folding Hinge

Step-by-step repair guide for a folding bike hinge that squeaks, binds, or won't align.

WARNING / SAFETY

  • Wear gloves and eye protection.
  • Support the bike securely before working.
  • Do not force misaligned hinge parts—check for debris or bent hardware.
  • If cracks or severe damage are found, stop and replace affected components.
