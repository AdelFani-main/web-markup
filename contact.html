FAQ

Tap a question to expand (wireframe behavior).

Brakes rubbing after folding?

Simple alignment checks

Check the caliper position and cable tension first.

🔧

Hinge clicking noise?

Clean it and use the right lube

A little lubricant helps, but do not overdo it—wipe off the excess.

⚙️

Gears skipping or not shifting smoothly?

Check the basics first

Look at the cable tension and the limit screws before changing anything else.

🧰

Flat tire on the road?

Patch or replace

Check the tube and rim first, then decide whether a patch or a new tube is the better fix.
