Chain slipped

Quick roadside steps to get the chain back on and keep the ride moving.

  1. Stop pedaling right away and keep the bike steady.
  2. Check the derailleur and look for anything jammed or misaligned.
  3. Put the chain back on the correct sprocket and make sure it sits snugly.

Flat tire

A simple roadside routine to remove the wheel, inspect the tube, and get back on the road.

  1. Stop somewhere safe and keep the bike steady before you touch the wheel.
  2. Take the wheel off and check the tube for the puncture.
  3. Patch or replace the tube, then inflate it to the right pressure.
