Chain Slipped

Urgent roadside steps to safely re-seat the chain and continue riding.

  1. Stop pedaling immediately and stabilize the bike.
  2. Inspect derailleur alignment and check for debris.
  3. Re-seat the chain onto the correct sprocket and verify tension.

Flat Tire

Urgent roadside steps to remove the wheel, inspect the tube, and get back on the road.

  1. Stop safely and stabilize the bike before touching the wheel.
  2. Remove the wheel and inspect the tube for the puncture area.
  3. Patch or replace the tube, then inflate to the recommended pressure.
