Essential items

Tick off the things you already have. Anything left over is what you’ll want to add to your kit.

  • Chain cleaner and degreaser

    Brush away grime before checking the drivetrain

    Use it, wipe it down, and keep things tidy.

  • Chain lube

    Choose dry or wet depending on the weather

    A light coat is enough—wipe off the excess.

  • Tire repair set

    Patches, tube, and tire levers

    Patch it, test it, and re-seat the tire carefully.

  • Brake adjustment tools

    Fine-tune alignment and tension

    Center the caliper and test the brakes before riding.

  • Allen key set

    Useful for fasteners on the frame and components

    Check the sizes before tightening anything down.

  • Multi-tool and spares

    Simple fixes when you’re out and about

    Include a spare tube and a couple of zip ties.
